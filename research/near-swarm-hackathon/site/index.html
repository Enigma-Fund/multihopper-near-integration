How it fits NEAR

An agent can finish its task while its customer keeps authority over payment. The NEAR contract tests separate job budgets, reject duplicate invoices and return unused synthetic funds after a failed job. MultiHopper’s Solana routing adds programmable payment timing and wallet screening. MultiHopper Revolver keeps assets segregated in dedicated chambers during Revolver execution. Signing a public work order records approval off-chain and leaves the assets in your wallet.
