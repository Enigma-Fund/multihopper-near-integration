Verify a screening receipt

Check a Solana transaction against its on-chain route ID. A match confirms that MultiHopper’s program accepted its screener’s proof for the route creator and ordered hop recipients.

Why this matters on NEAR

Suppose a customer hires two agents for one job. The researcher buys data. The reviewer pays for compute. If the researcher retries an invoice or the reviewer stops halfway through, who can still spend, and how much does the customer get back?

In our NEAR prototype, each agent has a separate account and the contract enforces its allowance. Our Solana workspace lets the customer sign the payout address and limits before work starts.

Benefits

  • Keep control of the terms. Your wallet signs the payout address, total budget and limit for each payment. Changing a signed amount invalidates the signature.
  • Keep agent budgets separate. In the NEAR tests, one agent can spend only its own allocation. MultiHopper Revolver keeps assets segregated in dedicated chambers during Revolver execution.
  • Retry an invoice safely. The NEAR payment tests show that submitting the same invoice twice pays it once.
  • Recover the unused balance. In a local NEAR test, one customer funded 100 synthetic units, spent 30 and recovered 70 after the job failed. A second customer’s 100 units remained intact.
  • Check the screening evidence. For an existing Solana route, a reviewer can check whether the program accepted the MultiHopper screener’s proof for the creator and ordered hop wallets.

Who it’s for

  • NEAR agent marketplaces and swarm builders can give each worker its own allowance within a customer’s job.
  • Teams hiring AI agents can set how much a researcher or reviewer may spend on data, APIs or compute.
  • Agent developers and service providers can bind a payment to an invoice and handle a retry without collecting twice.
  • Treasury and compliance teams can inspect the signed spending terms and check the screening evidence for a specific Solana route.
