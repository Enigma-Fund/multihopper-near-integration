Through our partnership with TRM Labs, we screen wallets on covered Solana asset transfers for AML and sanctions risks, including exposure to illicit funds. Our smart contract requires a valid screening proof before the route can advance.

MultiHopper gives each job fixed agent budgets, separate custody and a path to recover unused funds. Connect a wallet to sign in, then try payments and recovery with synthetic assets. Local NEAR mode uses a disposable chain.

What MultiHopper contributes

MultiHopper Revolver keeps assets segregated in dedicated chambers during Revolver execution. Each agent allocation has its own custody account and key. On-chain policies cap spending; the provider signs only the bound payment. A failed job revokes future grants and reconciles existing transactions before returning remaining principal. One customer never pays another customer's shortfall.

Fixed allocations, not a shared liquidity pool. Agents here are scripted test actors; work quality, bidding and dispute arbitration are not automated by this demo.
